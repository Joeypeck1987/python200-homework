1. Interest in the work.

## Reflection

Westport stands out as the closer match to a data engineering direction because
its pipeline and infrastructure responsibilities connect directly with the work
I am learning. If the location and experience requirements fit, it could balance
interesting work, remote access, and technical development. iSpot could also be
a useful route into hands-on data work, although its emphasis is analysis rather
than engineering. The higher listed compensation would not automatically make
it my first choice.

Right now, I would prioritize interesting work and a remote arrangement while
looking closely at how much support a new team member receives. In an interview,
I would ask who reviews my work, how onboarding is structured, and what someone
in this role could learn during the first year. Those answers would help me
distinguish a real growth opportunity from a long list of tools.

After gaining experience in my first technology job, I might give compensation
and specialization more weight. I would also have a better understanding of
which tasks I enjoy and where I want to deepen my skills. My first role can help
me build that understanding while giving me experience to support future moves.
